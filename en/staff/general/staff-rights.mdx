  # Staff Rights
</div>

<Note>
  **Important notice on rights flexibility (delegation and restrictions):** The higher project management (Owner, Co-Owner, Project Leadership, and authorized Managers) reserves the right to temporarily extend or restrict the permissions of lower-ranking staff members within reasonable limits to perform specific work tasks.

  **_Example:_** The Head RP Overseer may be temporarily granted the authority of the Head RP Overseer during the latter's absence to perform urgent tasks.

  **Warning:** The existence of this possibility does not grant the right to independently ask, beg, or demand additional permissions. Any unjustified requests for permission expansion (including those referencing this note) are strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action.
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="F-1 — Owner & CEO">
    - **F-1.1 — Core Duties and Functions:**
      - Strategic management of the project, servers, and staff.
      - Approval of monetization, budgets, and global development directions.
    - **F-1.2 — Permissions:**
      - Absolute authority: approval and modification of structure and rules.
      - Appointment and removal of any positions.
      - Veto right and revocation of any decisions.
      - No limitations on rights within the project.
    - **F-1.3 — Restrictions and Prohibitions:**
      - Sole personal accountability for the development and stability of the project.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="F-2 — CTO (Chief Technical Officer)">
    - **F-2.1 — Core Duties and Functions:**
      - Management of all technical infrastructure, databases, server architecture, codebase, and hosting stability.
    - **F-2.2 — Permissions:**
      - Full access to infrastructure, repositories, and databases.
      - Distribution of tasks to the technical department.
      - Approval of update schedules and release cycles.
    - **F-2.3 — Restrictions and Prohibitions:**
      - Financial decisions and server procurement only through the Owner.
      - Interference in the operational policy of other quadrants is prohibited.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="F-3 — COO (Chief Operating Officer)">
    - **F-3.1 — Core Duties and Functions:**
      - Operational management of all project quadrants.
      - Coordination of department heads, auditing regulations and KPI fulfillment.
    - **F-3.2 — Permissions:**
      - Exercise Owner's authority during their absence (except for removing the Owner).
      - Issuing operational instructions to department heads.
      - Auditing reports.
    - **F-3.3 — Restrictions and Prohibitions:**
      - Changes to the global project structure and overriding Owner decisions only with direct approval from the Owner.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="F-4 — CMO (Chief Marketing Officer)">
    - **F-4.1 — Core Duties and Functions:**
      - Strategic media planning, advertising campaigns, ad purchasing.
      - Coordination of blogger outreach, events, and external positioning.
    - **F-4.2 — Permissions:**
      - Management of the approved advertising budget.
      - Approval of media partners.
      - Moderation of information channels.
      - Appointment and removal of the PR Assistant.
    - **F-4.3 — Restrictions and Prohibitions:**
      - Deals exceeding the budget without CEO/COO approval are prohibited.
      - Interference in technical and HR regulations is prohibited.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="F-5 — Head Developer">
    - **F-5.1 — Core Duties and Functions:**
      - Leadership of the development team, technical expertise.
      - Task distribution, code review, deadline and quality control of system development.
    - **F-5.2 — Permissions:**
      - **Conduct technical expertise** and provide recommendations for improving project operations.
