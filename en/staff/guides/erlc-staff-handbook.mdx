### B-1.1 — General Criteria for Promotion

For successful career advancement within the staff, every member must meet the basic qualification standards. Any failure to meet these criteria is an automatic reason for denial of promotion.

<Note>
  - **Knowledge of the regulations:** Mandatory flawless knowledge of the vast majority of the rules and internal regulatory documents of the project.
  - **Impeccable reputation:** Absence of destructive behavior, conflicts within the team, and complaints from players.
  - **Disciplinary status:** Complete absence of active reprimands in the staff member's personal file.<br />
</Note>
