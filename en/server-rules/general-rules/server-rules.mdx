<div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-10">
  <h3 className="text-3xl font-bold">A. RP Rules</h3>
</div>

* **A-1** — The project has a separate section — [**RP Terms**](/en/server-rules/general-rules/rp-terms), containing standard roleplay violations and their abbreviations (e.g., RDM, VDM, LTAP, etc.), which are used throughout the roleplay sphere. Many of these terms may also be described in detail in the server rules. **A moderator has the right to reference them when issuing a penalty.**
  <Warning>
    **Important:** Due to the absence of text chat or its partial absence, transmitting information about a player's death (NLR) or location to a Discord voice channel is considered Metagaming.
  </Warning>
* **A-2** — It is prohibited to interfere with RP scenes in which you are not initially involved, including creating staged situations to draw you into a scene. If your involvement logically fits the RP scene and its participants are willing to involve you, this is not considered a violation.
  <Tip>
    **Example:** During an RP scene where terrorists are taking over a hospital, a player deliberately arrives at the scene as a journalist and starts interviewing the terrorists.
  </Tip>
* **A-3** — Conducting any events or large RP scenes, including car races, large checkpoints, motorcades, mass road blockades, and similar actions, is prohibited without prior permission from the staff on duty.
* **A-4** — Before putting handcuffs on someone, you must roleplay it.
  <Warning>
    **Note:** Since text chat is unavailable or not accessible to all players, roleplay is done by voice in the Discord scene or in the "game-chat" channel.
  </Warning>
  <Tip>
    **Example:** "I take the handcuffs out of the pouch and secure your hands behind your back."
  </Tip>
  <Danger>
    **Attention:** Performing an action without roleplay in the scene/channel is considered Non RP.
  </Danger>
* **A-5** — It is prohibited to participate in RP on behalf of organizations or factions that are currently inactive (even if the corresponding liveries exist for them).
* **A-6** — You may engage in any civilian work if it is realistic, appropriate, and does not violate other server rules. If buildings, vehicles, or other property of an active business are used for such activity, it must be carried out in compliance with the rules of that business.
* **A-7** — The real **[laws of the Russian Federation](https://www.consultant.ru/popular/)** apply on the server.
* **A-8** — It is prohibited to succumb to OOC provocations and to justify server rule violations with IC reasons. If players succumb to a provocation and violate the rules (engage in a shootout, NRP, OOC insults, etc.), the situation is not rolled back, and both sides receive punishment.
  <Note>
      **Note:** Legitimate procedural actions by government organization employees (identity check, detention, search, etc.) are a full IC process and are not considered an OOC provocation.
  </Note>
  <Warning>
      **Important:** If a player attempted to provoke a violation, but the other side did not succumb and submitted a report — only the provocateur receives punishment.
  </Warning>
  <Danger>
      **Warning:** The fact that a situation started within RP does not give the right to break server rules. If any RP conflict goes beyond what is permitted (mass shootout, RP disruption, resorting to OOC insults, etc.) — participants are punished on general grounds.
  </Danger>
* **A-9** — It is prohibited to evade punishment by any means — for example, leaving the server, not logging in, or voluntarily leaving a voice Mod Scene if the moderator has not yet dismissed you, as well as taking other actions to avoid responsibility.
* **A-10** — If a character is put on a wanted list, the player is not allowed to remove the wanted status within 10 minutes, leave the game or rejoin the server, and must continue roleplaying according to the situation. Attempting to bypass the rule (resetting the wanted status, changing character, etc.) is considered a violation and is punished at the discretion of moderation.
* **A-11** — Every player must be in a Discord scene while playing. The Discord nickname must match the in-game name. Using voice-changing software (except for special roles, e.g., characters with a unique role) or ignoring calls in the voice channel is equivalent to Non RP.

<div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-8">
  <h3 className="text-3xl font-bold">B. Factions</h3>
</div>

* **B-1** — It is prohibited to use standard ER:LC liveries (RCPD, LCSO, RCF&R, LCDOT) on any vehicles. On the server, only liveries provided by the server must be used. Use of a standard livery may result in a penalty.
* **B-2** — Liveries with the «Staff» inscription may only be used by the server staff.
* **B-3** — It is prohibited to deploy spikes or stop sticks at the last moment before an approaching vehicle, as well as under or near a parked car. Within RP scenes, for example during an interception, spikes must be deployed in advance.
* **B-4** — Impersonating any faction or department employees, including staff and other server teams you do not belong to, is prohibited. This includes unauthorized use of restricted service vehicles, displaying ranks or positions you do not hold in your display name, wearing uniforms you do not have access to, and copying departmental callsigns (e.g. `XX-000`).
* **B-5** — If you play as a faction that does not require joining and you are not part of its official roster, you must use exclusively neutral callsigns of strictly 4 digits with no letters or prefixes (e.g. `4832`), and remain in the basic uniform and in standard service vehicles.
* **B-6** — Uniform must match the vehicle you are driving (e.g., patrol uniform on a patrol vehicle).
* **B-7** — Faction leadership independently and within reasonable limits determines which transport and in what situations to use. All rules and restrictions must be published in the faction server public channels, and players are required to read and follow them.
* **B-8** — Unofficial DPS Officer Regulations.
  * **B-8.1** — An unofficial DPS officer is equivalent to the rank of Private in the hierarchy. They must unconditionally comply with the internal charter and obey the leadership not only of DPS but of the entire MVD.
  * **B-8.2** — Unofficial DPS employees are strictly prohibited from exceeding their legal authority. Any actions going beyond the established MVD regulations and legislation are punished according to server rules.
  * **B-8.3** — To perform their duties, unofficial DPS employees are allowed to use only weapons from the pistol category. The exception is the Model 29, the use of which is prohibited.
  * **B-8.4** — It is prohibited to play as an unofficial DPS employee if you are a member of any other governmental or criminal faction.
    <Note>
      **Exception:** This restriction does not apply to employees of EMERCOM, Ministry of Health, DepTrans, Investigative Committee, Prosecutor's Office, and Court.
    </Note>
    <Warning>
      **Special conditions for exceptions:** When playing as an unofficial DPS employee, representatives of the above factions are strictly prohibited from:

      1. Using their official powers and rights obtained in their primary faction (functionality, special equipment, etc.).
      2. Leaking any official information from their primary faction for unofficial activities or to third parties.
    </Warning>
    <Note>
      **Note:** The rule remains in effect for DPS employees themselves — you cannot be both an official and unofficial employee of the same structure. This does not apply to other unofficial factions.
    </Note>
* **B-9** — Use of the police helicopter is allowed **only upon request and approval in Discord leadership channels or with moderation approval**. Without official permission, helicopter use is prohibited.
* **B-10** — Displaying a rank or position in your display name is prohibited unless you are playing for the corresponding faction. Only allowed when in the faction and actively playing for it.
* **B-11** — Use of radios in the Discord "Civilian Operations." Use of radio communication channels (special voice Discord scenes "Radio") is only allowed for employees of the corresponding factions (MVD, EMERCOM, etc.).
* **B-12** — In the absence of active official lawyers, the right to temporarily act as one may be granted to any other player exclusively with direct approval from the server staff.
* **B-13** — Senior faction members have the right to roleplay as lower-ranking officers within the context of creating game situations.
  <Tip>
    **For example:** A Lieutenant General roleplays as a Sergeant to go on patrol, because Generals typically do not go on patrol.

  </Tip>

<div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-8">
  <h3 className="text-3xl font-bold">C. Criminal Activity</h3>
</div>

* **C-1** — Criminal actions are prohibited in the green zones indicated on the map below. In red zones, committing criminal actions is allowed at any time.

  <Accordion title="Safe Zones Map">
    <img src="/assets/server-rules/safe-zones-map.png" alt="Safe Zones Map" />
  </Accordion>

* **C-2** — It is prohibited to provoke law enforcement with reckless actions, such as traffic violations (e.g., running a STOP sign), reckless driving, false 112 calls, and other similar actions.
  * **C-2.1** — It is prohibited to join the Discord channel of an RP scene if you are not a participant in it, including for clarifications or to listen in on what is happening. Deliberate interference with the scene, creating distractions, as well as unjustified pings to players or faction employees aimed at provoking or creating conflict are prohibited.
  <Tip>
    **Example:** A player joins the Discord channel where an arrest is taking place and interferes with the conversation without being a participant of the scene — this is considered a violation of this rule.
  </Tip>
* **C-3** — Kidnappings are allowed exclusively for official organized crime groups for justified RP situations with a limit of once per 45 minutes for the entire organized crime group.
* **C-4** — During PT (Peace Timer), no intentional criminal actions are allowed, including chases, shootouts, robberies, kidnappings, etc.
* **C-5** — Groups of three or more people committing any illegal actions or displaying criminal behavior are considered unauthorized criminal groups and violate project rules.
  * **C-5.1** — Only officially registered criminal organizations (operating within Civil Operations) have the right to commit criminal actions as a group of three or more participants.
  * **C-5.2** — The maximum number of active members of one official organized crime group within a single game session directly depends on the total server online count. At the same time, only one operational group from one official organization may be present per session.

      | **Current Server Online** | **Max. Group Members** |
      | ------------------------- | ---------------------- |
      | 0 — 20 players            | up to 4 people         |
      | 20 — 25 players           | up to 5 people         |
      | 25 — 40 players           | up to 6 people         |
  * **C-5.3** — Joint actions or coordination between different groups, even within the same organized crime group, are prohibited during active situations.
    <Note>
      The behavior and activity of criminal groups are under constant staff supervision.
    </Note>
* **C-6** — Bank robberies are only allowed for official criminal organizations with at least 2 players participating.
  * **C-6.1** — There must be at least 10 minutes between bank robberies.
  * **C-6.2** — Jewelry store robberies can be conducted without belonging to an official organized crime group, but require at least 2 participants, and at least 10 minutes must pass between attempts.
  * **C-6.3** — All robberies must be conducted in roleplay format — no instant escapes and with proper roleplay (e.g., interaction with hostages, guards, police, etc.).
* **C-7** — Any players, including members of official criminal groups, can rob cash registers, ATMs, and jewelry stores.
  * **C-7.1** — All actions, including preparation and the robbery itself, must be fully roleplayed in "game-chat" — roleplay in RP scenes is not allowed.
  * **C-7.2** — Jewelry store robberies require heightened attention to roleplay realism.
  * **C-7.3** — Video recording of the robbery process is mandatory.
  * **C-7.4** — Do not forget rule A-10 — after a robbery, a 10-minute wanted period applies.

  <Accordion title="Roleplay Example">
    * \*Took the sports bag off shoulder\*
    * \*Threw the bag on the floor — bag is on the floor\*
    * \*Took out a hammer\*
    * \*Smashed the display with the hammer\*
    * \*Picks up jewelry with hands and puts it in the bag\*
    * \*Locked the bag\*
    * \*Put the bag on shoulder\*
  </Accordion>

* **C-8** — Only members of official criminal groups can participate in hostage-taking scenes. At least two captors must be actively involved in each such scene.
* **C-9** — High Rock Park cannot be used specifically to escape from law enforcement. Pursuit may begin in the park but cannot end/continue in the park after it has started.
* **C-10** — If all active members of an official organized crime group were **eliminated or arrested** simultaneously, that organized crime group **temporarily suspends its activity** and **is not allowed to roleplay for that organization for 1 hour** from the time of the incident.
    <Note>
      The organized crime group itself **is not removed from the Civil Operations server** — after 1 hour, participants may resume roleplaying with at least one active member.
    </Note>
    <Note>
      **Note:** "Active participants" refers to players currently in the organized crime group and participating in roleplay.
    </Note>
* **C-11** — It is prohibited to return to the organized crime group base during an active chase or shootout to hide from pursuit or receive support from your organization members (based on rule **A-2**).

<div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-8">
  <h3 className="text-3xl font-bold">D. Vehicles</h3>
</div>


* **D-1** — Vehicle operation must be realistic, and vehicle behavior and driving style must match road conditions and terrain. In mountainous areas and on difficult mountain dirt roads, it is prohibited to use sports cars, supercars and passenger cars (sedans, coupes, hatchbacks) — movement there is permitted only on SUVs, pickups and heavy special equipment (fire trucks, armored vehicles, trucks). Any vehicles are permitted on paved sections in the mountains, as well as on flat dirt roads (farm accesses, gentle cut-throughs). Climbing steep slopes and rocks off-road, as well as driving along the edges of the map, is prohibited for everyone.
* **D-2** — It is prohibited to honk without necessity, as well as to use the siren for more than 5–10 seconds without reason.
* **D-3** — Only bank employees may use bank trucks.
* **D-4** — Lawnmowers may only travel on grass and sidewalks. They must cross roadways exclusively at pedestrian crossings. Movement between zones must be carried out by transporting on a trailer.
* **D-5** — Information about prohibited vehicles and vehicles with usage restrictions can be found in the **📂┃information** channel on each of our game servers.
* **D-6** — ATVs and buggies may only be used within High Rock Park. During races, their use on closed road sections is permitted exclusively with the permission of law enforcement or server staff. The delivery of ATVs and buggies to the park, as well as their movement between permitted race locations, must be carried out on a trailer. The use of ATVs and buggies outside the park is prohibited.
* **D-7** — It is prohibited to take or spawn a vehicle within 10 minutes after the vehicle has been towed, including from the impound lot, in order to bypass the consequences of the tow. Any attempts to «reset» the tow by resetting or respawning the vehicle, selecting the car again, reconnecting to the server, or using any other methods are considered a violation.
* **D-8** — Forklifts are strictly prohibited from driving independently on public roads and city streets. Transportation of forklifts to the work site must be carried out exclusively on a tow truck or trailer. Operation is permitted only within the forklift work zones.


<div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-8">
  <h3 className="text-3xl font-bold">E. Dealing Damage</h3>
</div>

* **E-1** — Shooting, punching, knifing, etc. in a moderator scene, at staff, players, or any vehicle is prohibited.
* **E-2** — In case of severe injury or death (appearance of the respawn screen), respawning immediately is prohibited:
  * If there are medics on the server — you must call EMS and wait 5 minutes. If medics do not arrive within this time, respawning is permitted; if they arrive, continue RP.
  * If there are no medics on the server — wait 2 minutes, after which respawning is permitted.
* **E-3** — Organized crime groups are prohibited from using the weapons «Remington MSR», «Remington 700», «Remington 870», «PPSH-41» and «M249». The «AK-47» may only be used by organized crime groups officially registered on the server.
* **E-4** — «Orsis T 5000», «M4A1», «SPAS-12» and «G36C» may only be used by specialized units of factions. The decision to issue the weapon to a particular unit is made by the faction leadership, and the unit must be a specialized one.
* **E-5** — Jumping while shooting is prohibited.
* **E-6** — Every player, regardless of their role, must roleplay taking any weapon into their hands, whether it is a pistol, rifle, or shotgun.
  * Roleplay is allowed in the "game-chat" of the emergency services Discord channel or in the voice channel you are in (even if you are alone in it).
  * When using a voice channel, a video recording where your words are CLEARLY and loudly audible is mandatory.
  * Roleplay must be spoken strictly before shooting begins and must mention the specific type or name of the weapon, as well as the fact of bringing it into combat position (simply "took out a weapon" is not enough).
  <Note>
    **Important clarification:** roleplay is only required when taking a weapon into your hands, and putting it away does not require roleplaying the action.
  </Note>
* **E-7** — Employees of the Investigative Committee, the Prosecutor's Office and the Court are prohibited from carrying and using any firearms, as well as using special equipment: handcuffs, batons, rubber shotguns, tasers, assault shields, and battering rams. Use of individual body armor (bulletproof vests) of concealed carry only is permitted. Open carrying of combat gear and tactical equipment is strictly prohibited.
  <Note>
    **Exception for carrying firearms:** an introduced KTO (Counter-Terrorist Operation) regime.
  </Note>

<div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-8">
  <h3 className="text-3xl font-bold">F. Other</h3>
</div>

* **F-1** — All in-game actions must comply with the [**Roblox TOS**](https://wiki.erlcrussia.xyz/documentation-moscow-roleplay/general-pravila/rp-terminy#osnovnye-polozheniya-roblox-tos), otherwise a kick/ban will follow. This includes a ban on racism, 18+ roleplay, suicide, and drug roleplay.
* **F-2** — Being AFK for more than 5 minutes may result in a kick. Moderation is **not required** to formalize this kick.
* **F-3** — It is prohibited to use unrealistic avatar elements, such as shoulder pets, wings, swords, non-standard animations, unnatural skin tones, particles, and other accessories that do not fit the realistic style. The avatar body format must be **strictly the classic square R15 (no rounded parts)**. The use of any non-RP items is also prohibited — appearance and accessories must fully comply with realism principles.
* **F-4** — After a kick, joining the server is prohibited for 1 hour. Violating this rule may result in a temporary account ban.
* **F-5** — It is prohibited to roleplay as children or persons under 18 years of age.
* **F-6** — Setting up camp outside of a park or near a lake is prohibited.
* **F-7** — Ban for multi-accounting if created to evade punishments.
* **F-8** — Any business structure or Civil Operations structure during a single game session must maintain a player count balance. Exceeding this balance is considered a rule violation.
* **F-9** — Frequent rollback of intentionally performed roleplay actions, as well as rollback without the consent of other participants in the situation, is prohibited.
  * **F-9.1** — Situation rollback is only allowed with the consent of all participants in the RP scene or game chat. If at least one participant does not consent — rollback is prohibited. Exception: staff decision.
  * **F-9.2** — In case of a car accident or chase, failure to roleplay the accident without a rollback is considered a violation.
  * **F-9.3** — If three or more people are involved in a chase and a collision occurs, rollback is prohibited — all parties must roleplay the consequences.
  * **F-9.4** — If two players are in a chase and both continue moving after an accident, both are punished, regardless of who caused the collision.
  * **F-9.5** — An accidental car accident can be rolled back according to general rules — only with the consent of all participants.
  * **F-9.6** — If a car accident was caused by one player (e.g., hitting a pole or colliding with a wall), they must write or say the message ".// rollback" in "game-chat" or the RP scene.
  * **F-9.7** — After the start of official criminal proceedings against a player regarding a specific incident, "rollback" of this situation (annulling its consequences and progress) unilaterally or by mutual consent is prohibited.
    <Note>
      **Exception:** Rollback is possible exclusively with direct approval from Server Management (or Faction Curator/Chief Curator).
    </Note>
* **F-10** — It is prohibited to abuse in-game items such as graffiti, police tape, cones, barriers and other similar objects, as well as administrative props. It is prohibited to use them to block or restrict access to territories, doors, exits, roads, NPCs and other important in-game areas, as well as to interfere with players, staff or the conduct of RP scenes.

<div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-8">
  <h3 className="text-3xl font-bold">G. Voice Chats</h3>
</div>

* **G-1** — Soundpad, built-in Discord Soundboard, and similar programs are allowed exclusively for RP situations in RP scene channels (e.g., siren sounds, radio sounds, door opening sounds, working tool sounds).
  * **G-1.1** — Each sound effect — no more than 10 seconds, volume — no higher than 75 dB (or 75% in Discord Soundboard), so as not to drown out participants' voices.
  * **G-1.2** — Sound must match the current scene and not interfere with other players' dialogues.
  * **G-1.3** — Music (outside of a boombox), memes, trolling, profanity, loud background noises, animal cries, etc. are prohibited.
  * **G-1.4** — Any attempts to extend sound (combining multiple sounds, repeating the same effect in a row, layering) are prohibited. One effect — one pause. Any "tricks" are considered a violation and punished as interference with the RP process.
* **G-2** — Using a boombox is allowed in any game moment if it does not violate [**Roblox TOS**](https://wiki.erlcrussia.xyz/documentation-moscow-roleplay/general-pravila/rp-terminy#osnovnye-polozheniya-roblox-tos).
  * **G-2.1** — Use of any sounds and tracks that contradict Roblox rules is prohibited.
  * **G-2.2** — If a boombox in-game interferes with important communications in a Discord scene, you must turn it off or lower the volume upon first request from participants or staff.
  * **G-2.3** — Full responsibility for TOS compliance lies with the player.
* **G-3** — Violation of this rule is equivalent to interfering with the RP process and may result in removal from the channel or disciplinary action.
  * **G-3.1** — Presence in RP scene and radio voice channels is only allowed for players who are currently in the game on the server.
  * **G-3.2** — Any insult to a player (not a character!), touching on their nationality, appearance, or speech defects in a Discord scene is equivalent to harassment. RP character conflicts must not turn into personal player conflicts.
  * **G-3.3** — **Radio Channels**
    * **G-3.3.1** — These channels are IC (In Character) zones.
    * **G-3.3.2** — Observe hierarchy and professional tone. No shouting or unnecessary chatter.
    * **G-3.3.3** — The dispatcher (if present) has **priority mode** status. When they speak — everyone is silent.
  * **G-3.4** — **RP Scene Channels**
    * **G-3.4.1** — Use of OOC information (out-of-character messages) is only allowed with a prior warning — for example, with the note "OOC information" or "Non-RP information." Without a warning, the channel is strictly IC and used exclusively for roleplay. Any out-of-game conversations, off-topic discussions, technical discussions, and OOC conflicts between players are prohibited.
    * **G-3.4.2** — IC conflicts occurring within the roleplay process are allowed.
    * **G-3.4.3** — Respect other scene participants. Do not talk over each other, let your opponent finish speaking.
  * **G-3.5** — **Mod Scene Channels**
    * **G-3.5.1** — Only matters related to the given scene may be discussed. Any unrelated information is prohibited.
    * **G-3.5.2** — When a staff member speaks — all participants are silent.
    * **G-3.5.3** — Speak only when given the floor. Interrupting staff during proceedings is punishable by mute.
    * **G-3.5.4** — Be ready to enable screen sharing/send a recording link (the best option) upon staff request.
    * **G-3.5.5** — Any insults, personal attacks, or aggression will result in harsher punishment.
* **G-4** — If your character is in a "critical injury" state or killed, you must press the Mute button in Discord. You are prohibited from groaning, giving information, or commenting on enemy actions by voice until you appear at the hospital (respawn).
    <Note>
      It is allowed to speak OOC information regarding the severity of injury, location of injury, etc.
    </Note>
* **G-5** — Any scene participant can initiate a brief OOC pause with the phrase "One moment out of RP" if there is a technical glitch or suspicion of a severe rule violation. Abuse of pauses to stall for time or think of a response is prohibited.
* **G-6** — It is prohibited to deliver long monologues forcing action without giving the opponent a chance to physically interject in the Discord scene.\
  **Example:** "I tied you up-gagged you-took you away-killed you" said in 2 seconds without pauses is [PG](https://wiki.erlcrussia.xyz/documentation-moscow-roleplay/general-pravila/rp-terminy#powergaming). You must make pauses, giving the other side the opportunity to roleplay resistance or reaction.
* **G-7** — It is prohibited to impose RP situations of a sexual nature on players, torture with detailed sound descriptions, or any immoral actions that cause real discomfort to participants.
    <Warning>
      If a player says in the Discord scene "OOC: I find this unpleasant, please stop," the other player must stop behaving that way and tone it down.
    </Warning>

<div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-8">
  <h3 className="text-3xl font-bold">H. Staff</h3>
</div>

* **H-1** — It is prohibited to intentionally leave a proceeding (mod scene) by the following methods: leaving the server, resetting or intentionally killing the character, as well as any other actions that can interrupt the scene.
  * **H-1.1** — Leaving the voice channel where a situation review is being conducted before official permission from the moderator is equivalent to LTAMS and is punishable by a ban.
* **H-2** — It is prohibited to ignore staff requests or refuse to cooperate if the request has a justified reason and relates to you (for example, you are a participant in the scene, a witness, a suspected violator, or otherwise involved in the situation). In particular, upon staff request, you must provide any video recordings or screenshots related to the proceeding.
  * **H-2.1** — Refusing to join a voice channel to explain the situation is also considered refusal to cooperate.
* **H-3** — It is prohibited to intentionally interfere with a mod scene, deceive staff, or hinder their work. In case of inadequate behavior during a proceeding (e.g., insults or talking over), the player may be punished without prior warnings.
* **H-4** — It is prohibited to tow staff vehicles with a tow truck for the Department of Transport. Staff vehicles are an administrative tool and are not intended for interaction in gameplay.
* **H-5** — Complaints submitted without evidence (screenshots, video recordings, logs) or with truncated/incomplete recordings are not considered.
  * **H-5.1** — Complaints about violations committed **more than two days ago** are also not considered.
  * **H-5.2** — **If the violation is not obvious**, the video must **fully show the moment of violation itself, its beginning and end, as well as the context**, so that the fact can be unambiguously confirmed.
  * **H-5.3** — Intentional false reports against other players or project staff are prohibited and punishable by a ban.
* **H-6** — Correctly use `!mod` commands in **"Emergency Services" or the main Discord server** and avoid unnecessary requests to staff.
* **H-7** — Calling staff for refresh, respawn, and/or teleportation is prohibited. This also applies to other commands (change weather, grant/remove wanted status).
* **H-8** — If a staff vehicle is following you with flashing lights and a siren turned on, you are required to immediately stop and safely park on the shoulder without creating a traffic hazard.
* **H-9** — It is prohibited to intentionally mislead or deceive players and/or project staff, including spreading intentionally false information about rules, events, or staff actions, deceiving to gain advantages (in events, economy, businesses, roles, rewards, etc.), as well as false complaints, falsification of evidence, and distortion of facts. Penalty: ban until appeal.

**_The moderator decides which penalty to issue for a violation. The punishment depends on the severity of the violation._**
