### A-1 — RP Terms

The server uses standard roleplay terms and their abbreviations, such as RDM, VDM, Non RP, etc. Their meanings and the corresponding violations are described below.

<Accordion title="RP Terms">
  #### RDM (Random Death Match) — Killing without reason

  Killing one to three players without a significant roleplay reason.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** A player suddenly opens fire on an unarmed pedestrian for no reason.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 1 hour.
  </Danger>


  #### MRDM (Mass Random Death Match) — Mass killing without reason

  Killing or shooting at four or more players without reason within a short period of time.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** Runs into a crowd of civilians and starts shooting everyone one after another.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Ban until appeal.
  </Danger>


  #### SRDM (Staff Random Death Match) — Killing staff

  Killing one to three server staff members without a significant roleplay reason.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** Kills a moderator who is conducting an investigation or is on duty, without reason.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Ban until appeal.
  </Danger>


  #### MSRDM (Mass Staff Random Death Match) — Mass killing of staff

  Killing four or more staff members without a significant reason within a short period of time.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** Deliberately opens fire on a group of moderators or administrators.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Ban until appeal.
  </Danger>


  #### VDM (Vehicle Death Match) — Damaging vehicles

  Deliberately ramming other players’ cars, shooting at vehicles without reason or staging intentional accidents.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** A player deliberately crashes into cars parked near a service station at speed or shoots up their bodies.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 20 minutes.
  </Danger>


  #### MVDM (Mass Vehicle Death Match) — Mass vehicle damage

  Deliberate damage to four or more cars within a short time: mass ramming, provoking major traffic accidents or shooting at cars without reason.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** Speeds up on the oncoming lane and methodically smashes through a stream of cars or shoots at cars in the parking lot.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 30 minutes.
  </Danger>


  #### SVDM (Staff Vehicle Death Match) — Damaging staff vehicles

  Shooting at staff vehicles, deliberate ramming or staging traffic accidents involving them without a significant reason.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** Deliberately cuts off or crashes into an on-duty moderator’s car.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 30 minutes.
  </Danger>


  #### MSVDM (Mass Staff Vehicle Death Match) — Mass damage to staff vehicles

  Deliberate damage to four or more staff vehicles within a short time by any means.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** Shoots up or wrecks the administration’s official cars in the parking lot.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Kick.
  </Danger>


  #### FRP (Fear RolePlay) — Fear for one’s life

  Ignoring mortal danger and showing no natural fear for your character’s life. If you are faced with obvious danger or numerical inferiority, you must comply.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** A player tries to run away or acts defiant while three armed police officers are aiming at him point-blank.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 20 minutes.
  </Danger>


  #### NLR (New Life Rule) — New life after death

  After death the character forgets the circumstances of their death, the identity of the killer and has no right to take revenge.

  - You may return to the place of death only after 3 minutes (if a shootout, raid or robbery is still ongoing there, returning is prohibited until the situation fully ends).
  - The character remembers their friends, home and basic duties, but completely forgets the events that led to their death.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** Died in a shootout, respawned in the hospital and immediately rushed back to kill his offender.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 20 minutes.
  </Danger>


  #### MR (Medical Rushing) — Rushing medical care

  Providing medical care unrealistically quickly by ambulance staff without proper roleplay.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** A medic ran up, instantly picked up the wounded with a button and shoved them into the ambulance without any first aid roleplay.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Warning.
  </Danger>


  #### FTC (Failure To Comply) — Refusing orders

  Refusing to carry out lawful requests of police officers within RP or direct instructions from an on-duty moderator.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** A player ignores a moderator’s request to remove a prohibited vehicle or refuses to turn off the engine on a traffic police officer’s order.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 40 minutes.
  </Danger>


  #### MG (Metagaming) — Use of out-of-game information

  Using any out-of-game information (from Discord, streams, private messages) that your character could not have accessed in the game, in order to gain an advantage.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** A friend told you on Discord voice where the criminal is hiding or where an ambush is set up, and you drove there following their tip.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 40 minutes.
  </Danger>


  #### PG (Powergaming) — Attributing unrealistic abilities

  Attributing unrealistic superpowers to your character, playing “terminator”, as well as actions that leave the opponent no chance to react.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** Roleplay like “kicked them in the jaw — knocked them out cold”, without giving the other player a chance to dodge or defend.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 20 minutes.
  </Danger>


  #### NonRP (Non Role-Play) — Unrealistic behaviour

  Any behaviour that breaks the roleplay atmosphere: deliberate clowning, leaving the game to escape arrest or going AFK in an active scene.

  - **Atmosphere:** it is forbidden to turn the game into a circus. The main language of communication is Russian (short phrases in other languages are allowed). Voice-chat roleplay is only allowed if voice is available for all participants of the scene and you are making a video recording.
  - **Playing in your own favour:** you cannot roleplay actions solely to guarantee a win or react to something your character could not physically have seen.
  - **Provocation:** it is forbidden to intentionally bait players and government structures into aggression and chases without a logical IC reason.

  <Tip>
    **_Example:_** Bursts into someone else’s arrest, jumps across the roofs of official cars and shouts incoherent phrases.
  </Tip>

  <Danger>
    **_Punishment:_** Kick.
  </Danger>


</Accordion>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Specified separately for each term — look at the list above.
</Danger>

### A-2 — Interfering with Others’ RP Scenes

It is prohibited to interfere with RP scenes in which you are not initially involved, including creating staged situations to draw you into a scene. If your involvement logically fits the RP scene and its participants are willing to involve you, this is not considered a violation.

<Tip>
  **_Example:_** During an RP scene where terrorists are taking over a hospital, a player deliberately arrives at the scene as a journalist and starts interviewing the terrorists.
</Tip>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 10 minutes.
</Danger>

### A-3 — Events and Large RP Scenes

Conducting any events or large RP scenes, including car races, large checkpoints, motorcades, mass road blockades, and similar actions, is prohibited without prior permission from the staff on duty.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning.
</Danger>

### A-4 — Handcuffing Roleplay

Before putting handcuffs on someone, you must roleplay it.

<Warning>
  **_Note:_** Since text chat is unavailable or not accessible to all players, roleplay is done by voice in the Discord scene or in the "game-chat" channel.
</Warning>
<Tip>
  **_Example:_** \*took the handcuffs out of the pouch and secured the suspect's hands behind their back\*
</Tip>
<Danger>
  **_Attention:_** Performing an action without roleplay in the scene/channel is considered Non RP.
</Danger>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 10 minutes.
</Danger>

### A-5 — Civilian Work

You may engage in any civilian work if it is realistic, appropriate, and does not violate other server rules (such as Non RP or Roblox Community Standards). If buildings, vehicles, or other property of an active business are used for such activity, it must be carried out in compliance with the rules of that business.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 10 minutes.
</Danger>

### A-6 — Applicable Laws

The real [**laws of the Russian Federation**](https://www.consultant.ru/popular/) apply in RP. It is prohibited to ignore them or to claim in-game that they do not work.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** A note in the punishment history.
</Danger>

### A-7 — OOC Provocation

It is prohibited to succumb to OOC provocations and to justify server rule violations with IC reasons. If players succumb to a provocation and violate the rules (engage in a shootout, NRP, OOC insults, etc.), the situation is not rolled back, and both sides receive punishment.

<Note>
    **_Note:_** Legitimate procedural actions by government organization employees (identity check, detention, search, etc.) are a full IC process and are not considered an OOC provocation.
</Note>
<Warning>
    **_Important:_** If a player attempted to provoke a violation, but the other side did not succumb and submitted a report — only the provocateur receives punishment.
</Warning>
<Danger>
    **_Warning:_** The fact that a situation started within RP does not give the right to break server rules. If any RP conflict goes beyond what is permitted (mass shootout, RP disruption, resorting to OOC insults, etc.) — participants are punished on general grounds.
</Danger>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 40 minutes.
</Danger>

### A-8 — Evading Punishment

It is prohibited to evade punishment by any means — for example, leaving the server, not logging in, or voluntarily leaving a voice Mod Scene if the moderator has not yet dismissed you, as well as taking other actions to avoid responsibility.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Ban for 7 days.
</Danger>

### A-9 — 10-Minute Wanted Rule

If a character is put on a wanted list, the player is not allowed to remove the wanted status within 10 minutes, leave the game or rejoin the server, and must continue roleplaying according to the situation. Attempting to bypass the rule (resetting the wanted status, changing character, etc.) is considered a violation and is punished at the discretion of moderation.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 30 minutes.
</Danger>

### A-10 — Discord Scene and Nickname

Every player must be in a Discord scene while playing. The Discord nickname must match the in-game name. Using voice-changing software (except for special roles, e.g., characters with a unique role) or ignoring calls in the voice channel is equivalent to Non RP.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 15 minutes.
</Danger>

### A-11 — Deploying Spikes and Stop Sticks

It is prohibited to deploy spikes or stop sticks at the last moment before an approaching vehicle, as well as under or near a parked car. Within RP scenes, for example during an interception, spikes must be deployed in advance.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 30 minutes.
</Danger>

### A-12 — Evading Arrest

It is prohibited to intentionally evade an arrest, a pursuit or detention (including while handcuffed) by leaving the game, rejoining the server or respawning.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 25 minutes.
</Danger>

### B-1 — Impersonating Other Members

Impersonating any faction or department employees, including staff and other server teams you do not belong to, is prohibited. This includes unauthorized use of restricted service vehicles, displaying ranks or positions you do not hold in your display name, wearing uniforms you do not have access to, and copying departmental callsigns (e.g. `XX-000`).

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning.
</Danger>

### B-2 — Neutral Callsigns and Uniform

If you play as a faction that does not require joining and you are not part of its official roster, you must use exclusively neutral callsigns of strictly 4 digits with no letters or prefixes (e.g. `4832`), and remain in the basic uniform and in standard service vehicles.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** A note in the punishment history with a requirement to change your callsign.
</Danger>

### B-3 — Uniform Matching the Vehicle

Uniform must match the vehicle you are driving (e.g., patrol uniform on a patrol vehicle).

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** A note in the punishment history with a requirement to change your uniform.
</Danger>

### B-4 — Faction Vehicles

Faction leadership independently and within reasonable limits determines which transport and in what situations to use. All rules and restrictions must be published in the faction server public channels, and players are required to read and follow them.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** A note in the punishment history with a requirement to change the vehicle.
</Danger>

### B-5 — Unofficial DPS Officer Regulations

  - **B-5.1** — An unofficial DPS officer is equivalent to the rank of Private in the hierarchy. They must unconditionally comply with the internal charter and obey the leadership not only of DPS but of the entire MVD.

    <Danger>
      **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 10 minutes.
    </Danger>

  - **B-5.2** — Unofficial DPS employees are strictly prohibited from exceeding their legal authority. Any actions going beyond the established MVD regulations and legislation are punished according to server rules.

    <Danger>
      **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 15 minutes.
    </Danger>

  - **B-5.3** — To perform their duties, unofficial DPS employees are allowed to use only weapons from the pistol category. The exception is the Model 29, the use of which is prohibited.

    <Danger>
      **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 10 minutes.
    </Danger>

  - **B-5.4** — It is prohibited to play as an unofficial DPS employee if you are a member of any other governmental or criminal faction.

    <Note>
      **_Exception:_** This restriction does not apply to employees of EMERCOM, Ministry of Health, DepTrans, Investigative Committee, Prosecutor's Office, and Court.
    </Note>
    <Warning>
      **_Special conditions for exceptions:_** When playing as an unofficial DPS employee, representatives of the above factions are strictly prohibited from:

      1. Using their official powers and rights obtained in their primary faction (functionality, special equipment, etc.).
      2. Leaking any official information from their primary faction for unofficial activities or to third parties.
    </Warning>
    <Note>
      **_Note:_** The rule remains in effect for DPS employees themselves — you cannot be both an official and unofficial employee of the same structure. This does not apply to other unofficial factions.
    </Note>

    <Danger>
      **_Punishment:_** Warning.
    </Danger>

### B-6 — Police Helicopter

Use of the police helicopter is allowed **only upon request and approval in Discord leadership channels or with moderation approval**. Without official permission, helicopter use is prohibited.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 15 minutes.
</Danger>

### B-7 — Ranks and Titles in Nicknames

Displaying a rank or position in your display name is prohibited unless you are playing for the corresponding faction. Only allowed when in the faction and actively playing for it.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** A note in the punishment history with a requirement to change your nickname.
</Danger>

### B-8 — "Radio" Voice Channels

The "Radio" voice channels are intended only for faction staff on duty and are a strict IC zone. Hierarchy and a professional tone must be observed; any shouting or unnecessary chatter is prohibited. The dispatcher (if present) has priority mode status, so everyone must remain silent while they speak.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning.
</Danger>

### B-9 — Acting as a Lawyer

In the absence of active official lawyers, the right to temporarily act as one may be granted to any other player exclusively with direct approval from the server staff.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 10 minutes for playing without permission.
</Danger>

### B-10 — Roleplaying Lower Ranks

Senior faction members have the right to roleplay as lower-ranking officers within the context of creating game situations.

<Tip>
  **_For example:_** A Lieutenant General roleplays as a Sergeant to go on patrol, because Generals typically do not go on patrol.

</Tip>

### B-11 — Inactive Organizations and Factions

It is prohibited to participate in RP on behalf of organizations or factions that are currently inactive (even if the corresponding liveries exist for them).

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning.
</Danger>

### B-12 — Player Balance of Civilian Operations Structures

Civilian Operations structures are prohibited from disrupting the player balance during a gameplay session.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning.
</Danger>

### C-5 — Bank Robberies

Bank robberies are allowed exclusively for official OPGs with at least two players participating and a mandatory break of at least 10 minutes between attempts. All actions must be conducted strictly in roleplay format in the "game-chat" channel — without instantly escaping and with proper roleplay (e.g., interaction with hostages, guards, police, etc.). Roleplay in "RP scenes" is not permitted. After the robbery, a 10-minute wanted period applies under rule A-9.

  - \*the bag is completely stuffed with money\*
  - \*zipped up the bag and slung it over the shoulder\*
  - \*signalled his accomplices and headed for the exit\*

### C-8 — High Rock Park

It is prohibited to intentionally drive into High Rock Park in order to escape the police. A pursuit may start inside the park, but the offender must leave it immediately.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 20 minutes.
</Danger>

### C-9 — Water Transport and Spawning Boats

It is prohibited to intentionally spawn a boat during a pursuit in order to escape the police. Escaping by water is allowed only if the boat was spawned in advance before the pursuit began, or if the pursuit itself started on the water.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 20 minutes.
</Danger>

### C-10 — Complete Elimination of the OCG

If all active members of an official organized crime group were **eliminated or arrested** simultaneously, that organized crime group **temporarily suspends its activity** and **is not allowed to roleplay for that organization for 1 hour** from the time of the incident.

<Note>
  The organized crime group itself **is not removed from the Civil Operations server** — after 1 hour, participants may resume roleplaying with at least one active member.
</Note>
<Note>
  **_Note:_** "Active participants" refers to players currently in the organized crime group and participating in roleplay.
</Note>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 1 hour.
</Danger>

### C-11 — Returning to the OCG Base

It is prohibited to return to the organized crime group base during an active chase or shootout to hide from pursuit or receive support from your organization members.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 30 minutes.
</Danger>

### D-1 — Realistic Vehicle Control

Vehicle operation must be realistic, and vehicle behavior and driving style must match road conditions and terrain. In mountainous areas and on difficult mountain dirt roads, it is prohibited to use sports cars, supercars and passenger cars (sedans, coupes, hatchbacks) — movement there is permitted only on SUVs, pickups and heavy special equipment (fire trucks, armored vehicles, trucks). Any vehicles are permitted on paved sections in the mountains, as well as on flat dirt roads (farm accesses, gentle cut-throughs).

<Note>
  **_Note:_** Climbing steep slopes and rocks off-road, as well as driving along the edges of the map, is prohibited for everyone.
</Note>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning.
</Danger>

### D-2 — Horns and Sirens

It is prohibited to honk without necessity, as well as to use the siren for more than 5–10 seconds without reason.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** A note in the punishment history with a requirement to stop.
</Danger>

### D-3 — Bank Trucks

Only bank employees may use bank trucks.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning.

The use of ATVs and buggies outside the park is prohibited.

<Danger>

### E-2 — Time Before Respawn

Upon severe injury or death (when the respawn banner appears), respawning immediately is prohibited. If there are medics on the server, call an ambulance and wait 5 minutes; if there are none — wait 2 minutes without calling, after which respawning is allowed. Your Discord microphone must remain muted for the whole time — any conversations, groans, or passing information about the death and coordinates over voice are considered Metagaming and a violation of NLR.

  **_Note:_** It is allowed to share OOC information about the severity of the injury, the location of the injury, etc.
</Note>

  **_Punishment:_** Demorgan for 20 minutes.

It is prohibited to use the weapons «Remington MSR», «Remington 700», «Remington 870», «PPSH-41», «M249» and «AK-47».

### G-1 — Sound Effects in RP Scenes

Soundpad, the built-in Discord Soundboard and similar programs are allowed exclusively for RP situations in RP scene channels. Each sound effect in Soundpad must last no more than 10 seconds and the volume must not exceed 75 dB. The sound must match the current scene and must not interfere with the dialogue of other players. Any attempts to bypass the duration limit by stitching, repeating or overlapping several sounds are prohibited.

<Tip>
  **_For example:_** sounds of sirens, radios, opening doors, working tools, etc.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  **_Important:_** Using music that is not played through the in-game boombox is prohibited, as well as memes, trolling, profanity, loud background noise, animal screams and other sounds unrelated to the current RP scene are prohibited.

The boombox is allowed in any gameplay moment, as long as the tracks and sounds do not violate the [**Roblox Community Standards**](https://about.roblox.com/community-standards). RP scene channels are a strict IC zone and are intended exclusively for roleplay. Talking over each other, unrelated conversations and any disputes between players are prohibited inside the channels. Roleplay conflicts between characters are allowed as long as they do not turn personal.

<Warning>

<Danger>

Any scene participant can announce a brief OOC pause with the phrase "One moment OOC" or "OOC pause" when a technical glitch or a gross violation of the rules occurs. Abusing pauses to stall for time, to escape RP or to think over your actions is prohibited.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning.

It is prohibited to deliver long monologues forcing action without giving the opponent a chance to physically interject in the Discord scene. You must make pauses, giving the other side the opportunity to roleplay resistance or reaction. A monologue delivered without pauses is equated with [PG](/en/server-rules/general-rules/server-rules#pg-powergaming-—-attributing-unrealistic-abilities).

<Tip>

<Danger>

<Danger>
